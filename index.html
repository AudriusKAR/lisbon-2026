C · Daily Guide

🛬 Tuesday 10-20 — Arrival + Chiado
  1. Airport → Chiado. Best for 4 people with luggage: Uber/Bolt XL (~€15–25) or taxi (~€15–20 + €1.60 luggage fee), 15–25 min. The Aerobus no longer exists. Metro is cheaper (~€8 for all) but involves a transfer and very deep escalators — awkward with bags. Cars can't enter the narrowest lanes — you'll be dropped on Rua da Misericórdia.
  2. Check in at the WHome apartment. Confirm the check-in time with the host once we know the flight.
  3. Orientation loop on foot (~2–3 h, all downhill): Praça Luís de Camões (2 min) → Rua Garrett / Chiado → Baixa / Rua Augusta → Praça do Comércio and the riverfront.
  4. Dinner in Bairro Alto / Chiado — spontaneous (flexible day, depends on arrival time). Quick option: Time Out Market at Cais do Sodré (everyone picks a different dish).
  5. On the way back — evening view from Miradouro de São Pedro de Alcântara (5 min from home).
🏰 Wednesday 10-21 — Alfama + Castle + 🔒 Boat Party
  1. 09:30 — head out. Walk through Baixa to Alfama (~20 min) or tuk-tuk uphill. Avoid tram 28 at peak times — queues + pickpockets.
  2. Miradouro de Santa Luzia → the narrow lanes of Alfama.
  3. ~11:00 — Castelo de São Jorge: €17, in October 09:00–21:00, NO advance slot needed (tickets on site or castelodesaojorge.pt). Allow ~1.5–2 h — views over the whole city.
  4. 13:30 — lunch in Alfama or Baixa, 15:00–16:30 rest at home (long evening ahead).
  5. 16:45 — leave. Route downhill: Rua Garrett → Rua Augusta → through the arch into Praça do Comércio → SE corner. Route on Google Maps (~1.2 km, 15–18 min downhill).
  6. 17:05 — Estação Fluvial Sul e Sueste (white building with the clock). ⚠️ Check-in is INSIDE the building, "Pacific Cruises" ticket booth 1, NOT at the pontoon the boat leaves from. €1.30/pp tax on the spot.
  7. 17:45–19:45 — 🔒 Sunset Boat Party. DJ, beer, wine, sangria, open bar. Sunset at 18:50 — right in the middle of the cruise. It gets chilly on the water — bring a jacket!
  8. ~20:15 — dinner. Recommendation: Peixola (Rua do Alecrim 35) — 4.7★ Google, modern seafood, €25–50/pp, open till 23:00, directly on the walk home (14–16 min from the pier, 7 min from home afterwards). Book via TheFork. Backups: Palácio Chiado (spectacular 18th-c. palace interior, but uneven food, pricey) · Marisqueira Uma (arroz de marisco, closest to the pier) · O Velho Eurico (best value in town, but tables must be hunted weeks ahead via IG @ovelhoeurico).

Nothing serious after 19:45 — no museums or sightseeing, just dinner and Bairro Alto.

🚂 Thursday 10-22 — Sintra (Pena + Regaleira)

Verdict: Thursday is one of the BEST days for Sintra. Weekends are packed, and Mondays are surprisingly busy too (Lisbon museums close — everyone goes to Sintra); Tue–Thu are quietest. Neither Pena nor Regaleira closes on Thursdays.

  1. 07:20 — leave for Rossio station (10–12 min). Best to buy the Navegante cards (€0.50 each + fares, 4 separate cards needed) the evening before.
  2. 07:41 — train to Sintra (40 min, ~€2.45). ⚠️ The 08:11 train does NOT work — it misses the first bus and the 10:00 slot collapses. Zero-stress option: 07:11.
  3. On arrival at 08:21 — straight to the 434 bus queue (first bus ~08:50, hop-on ticket ~€11–13.50, sintra434.com). If the queue is too long — tuk-tuk or Uber (~€10–15).
  4. 09:15 — through the Pena park gate. Gate to palace is a 30-min uphill walk (official figure) or the internal shuttle (~€4.50).
  5. 10:00 — ⚠️ Pena Palace slot. ZERO tolerance: arrive late and the ticket is lost; early entry not allowed. "Essential Visit" ticket €20/pp only via bilheteira.parquesdesintra.pt. In October the popular morning slots sell out days ahead — buy 2–3 weeks before. Safer alternative: take 10:30.
  6. 12:30 — 434 down to Sintra centre → lunch (~1.5 h).
  7. ~15:00 — Quinta da Regaleira (10–12 min walk from the centre): €20/pp, slots every 30 min with a 1-hour grace period, last slot 16:30, gardens open till 18:30. Tickets: regaleira.byblueticket.pt. The Initiation Well!
  8. 17:30–18:00 — walk back to the station (~15 min) → trains every ~20 min → Rossio.
  9. Free evening. We do NOT overload the Sintra day — Pena + the centre + Regaleira is the quality maximum.
🗼 Friday 10-23 — Belém + free afternoon + Fado

💳 The Lisboa Card 24h (€31/pp, shop.visitlisboa.com) pays off exactly for this day: Jerónimos €18 + Torre €15 + Padrão €10 + transport ~€3 = €46 → saves ~€15/pp. It does NOT cover the Sintra palaces, so the 72h version isn't worth it. Activate on Friday morning.

  1. 08:05 — walk down to Cais do Sodré (8–10 min via Rua do Alecrim).
  2. 08:20 — CP train → Belém (7 min, ~€1.55; some fast Cascais trains skip Belém — check the departure board). ⚠️ Avoid tram 15E: 25–35 min and the city's most pickpocket-prone line.
  3. 08:30 — Pastéis de Belém (Rua de Belém 84): the street queue is takeaway only; go inside to the dining rooms (1,000+ seats, wait usually <10 min). Warm pastéis + coffee.
  4. 09:30 — Mosteiro dos Jerónimos opening slot (€18 or Lisboa Card; timed tickets via mmp.bymeoblueticket.pt). Monastery + cloister ~1.5 h. The church is free.
  5. 11:15 — across Praça do Império to the river: Padrão dos Descobrimentos (€10 / free with Lisboa Card) — lift to the 56 m terrace, the best panorama of Belém.
  6. ~12:15 — riverside walk to Torre de Belém. Reopened 2026-05-27 after restoration; interior now has slots (60 people/30 min, 900/day, €15). The interior is modest — if no slots are left, viewing from outside is a perfectly respectable plan.
  7. 13:00 — on the way back, the MAAT roof walk (free, 10 min) → train back → lunch near Cais do Sodré / Chiado.
  8. 14:30–19:30 — FREE (deliberately ≥3 h): cafés, souvenirs, Manteigaria nata for comparison, miradouros, rest.
  9. ~20:00 — FADO. Three verified options:
    1) O Faia (Rua da Barroca 54, 3 min from home) — classic fado house since 1947 with dinner and first-rank singers, ~€65–90/pp, the dressy option. Reservation essential: ofaia.com, +351 213 426 742.
    2) A Baiuca (Rua de São Miguel 20, Alfama, ~10 min by taxi) — fado vadio between the tables, home cooking, ~€25–35/pp minimum, on the Fado Museum's official route. Book by phone: +351 218 867 284.
    3) A Tasca do Chico (Rua do Diário de Notícias 39, 2 min — around the corner!) — fado vadio tavern since 1994, no cover charge, drinks + petiscos only (~€10–20). Music from 21:00 till 03:00. For 4 people book via WhatsApp +351 961 339 696.
    Recommended combo: dinner with fado at O Faia OR A Baiuca → late-night stop at Tasca do Chico on the way home.
    Avoid: Café Luso (tour-bus groups, inflated prices) and "Fado in Chiado" (a staged show for tourists).
🛫 Saturday 10-24 — Departure
  1. Flight is 🔒 booked, time TBD — until we know it, the morning stays light.
  2. Breakfast / brunch in Chiado, last stroll, souvenirs (e.g. tinned-fish shops, Vista Alegre, A Vida Portuguesa on Rua Anchieta).
  3. Agree the check-out time with the host; ask about luggage storage.
  4. Uber XL / taxi to the airport (15–25 min without traffic; Saturdays are calmer). Be at the airport 2.5 h before the flight.
  5. Note: that night (10-24→25) the clocks change — only relevant if the flight slips to Sunday. A Jungle concert is possibly on at MEO Arena on the evening of 10-24 (doesn't interfere, unless you'd like to go — VERIFY).
